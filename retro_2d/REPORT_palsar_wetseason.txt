REPORT - Preceding wet-season rain at each PALSAR-2 date vs PALSAR and k3r / k4r maps
=====================================================================================

Status: complete, 2026-10-31. User request: wetter seasons give new leaves and larger
plants, not only moisture; relate each year to the rain of the PRECEDING WET SEASON,
not only the 30 days before. Follows REPORT_palsar_date.txt (same seams, same ground).


ANSWER
------

1. The preceding (completed) wet season does NOT show in PALSAR or in the classes.
   - Seams: net step vs difference in W_prev, rho -0.20 to +0.11 (23 seams, n.s.).
   - Year pairs (same land, two dates): rho -0.03 to +0.28 (29 groups, n.s.).
   - Years (n = 5): rho -0.7 to +0.6, all n.s.
   The two clean cases (same incidence, large W_prev difference) are null:
     2023-12-06 | 2022-12-21: W_prev +676 mm -> HV +0.05 SD, k3r code +0.02
     2024-07-31 | 2024-01-03: W_prev -623 mm -> HV +0.09 SD, k3r code +0.09 (wrong sign)

2. What DOES show is rain in the CURRENT wet season before the date (W_curr). This is
   non-zero only for dates inside the wet season (Dec-Mar here).
   - Year pairs: HV rho +0.69, HH +0.64, alpha +0.61, k3r code +0.58, k4r +0.62
     (all p <= 0.001, 29 groups).
   - Seams: HV +0.47, alpha +0.48, k3r code +0.46, k4r +0.51 (p 0.01-0.03, 23 seams).
     Same-incidence seams only: +0.13 to +0.28, n.s. (n = 15).

3. The effect goes away again the next dry season. Same land (Biota track / South
   Dampier / YA25_1-2 strip), same beam (linci 32 deg in all three):
     2021-10-27 (dry) -> 2023-03-29 (W_curr 897 mm): HV +0.79 SD, alpha +1.73 SD, k3r code +0.30
     2023-03-29       -> 2024-08-28 (dry):            HV -0.64 SD, alpha -1.55 SD, k3r code -0.35
   This is the pattern of a temporary wet-season state (water in soil and plants, green
   leaves, grass). It is not the pattern of growth that stays.

4. Interpretation. At L-band (24 cm) the backscatter is mostly from stems and branches.
   New leaves and grass are small to L-band and are mostly lost by the dry season, when
   most strips are acquired. One season of woody growth is too small to see. So the leaf
   and plant response that the user expects is real, but in PALSAR it appears only as
   the in-season effect (2), and it does not carry into the dry season (1, 3).
   S1 and S2 (July images) are a different question: REPORT_rain.txt found that S2 bands
   follow the wet season (rho -0.9). That is not tested here.

5. "Preceding wet season" is not the same for every pixel of a mosaic. For 2023 and 2024
   it differs from the Jul-Jun rain year in REPORT_rain.txt:
     year  W_prev at own dates  rain-year wet (REPORT_rain)
     2019  198                  174
     2021  888                  921
     2023  688                  1036
     2024  531                  283     (Jan 2024 strips follow the wet 2022-23 season)
     2025  561                  598

6. Years (n = 5): k3r mean code vs rain30 rho -1.0 (p 0.02) and k4r C3 vs W_curr -1.0
   (p 0.02): 2 hits in 68 tests, expected about 3 by chance. The sign is opposite to the
   within-year result. Not used.

Summary: the class maps do not follow the preceding wet season. They follow rain in the
current wet season when the acquisition is inside it, and that effect reverses in the
next dry-season image. For the class maps this is an acquisition artefact. Wet-season
strips (2019-02-01, 2022-12-21, 2023-03-01, 2023-03-29) read taller than the same land in
a dry season.

METHOD
------

Script     retro_2d\palsar_wetseason.py (log palsar_wetseason.log)
Rain       ERA5 monthly total precipitation, 0.25 deg (outputs\rain\raw\era5_tp_monthly_DP.nc,
           2000-01..2025-12; the csv of REPORT_rain stops at 2025-07, the raw file does not).
           Each fixed-ground pixel takes its nearest ERA5 cell (58 cells) and its own date.
           W_prev  Oct-Apr total of the last wet season completed before the date
           W_curr  1 Oct of the current season to the date (0 for May-Sep dates)
           R12     the 12 full months before the date's month
           rain30  30 days before the date, monthly pro rata
Seams      palsar_date seams (same pixel sets, same net steps); rain = side a mean - side b mean.
Year pairs consecutive image years; pixels grouped by (strip in year 1, strip in year 2),
           groups >= 0.5 % of fixed ground. Group change = median band change (pooled SD)
           and mean class-code change. Each pair's share-weighted mean is removed (whole-year
           model and acquisition offset), so the test is within each pair.
Years      fixed-ground means of the pixel rain values, exact permutation p.


CAVEATS
-------

1. Few clean cases for W_prev: a large W_prev difference at a seam happens only where a
   strip from Jan-Mar sits next to one from later in the year (2019, 2023, 2024). 6 seams.
2. W_curr and incidence are confounded at the 2024-11-25 seams (W2 beam, +10-12 deg);
   with the same incidence the seam result is not significant. The year-pair result and
   the Biota-strip case (3) have the same incidence and support W_curr.
3. Year-pair groups are not independent (shared strips) and a group's change includes
   any real change (fire) in that area. p values are optimistic.
4. ERA5 at 0.25 deg; monthly totals; the wet season is fixed at Oct-Apr.
5. The PALSAR calibration caveat in REPORT_palsar_date.txt (DN amplitude vs DN/10000)
   applies to all bands here.
6. Only PALSAR and the class maps are tested; S1 / S2 are July images on other dates.
