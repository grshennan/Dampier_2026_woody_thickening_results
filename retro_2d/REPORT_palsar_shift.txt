REPORT - PALSAR-2 histogram shift 2019-2025 vs k3r / k4r class shares
======================================================================

Status: complete, 2026-10-31. User request: histograms of each year's PALSAR, clipped to the Dampier
coastline; is there a shift; if so, does it relate to the class-share change between years.
Maps used: k4r and k3r, one model trained on each year's own data. THIS IS THE STANDARD RESULT NOW
(user, 2026-10-31). The _m2025 transfer maps are not used.


ANSWER
------

1. Yes, there is a shift. It is small in gamma0 and large in the decomposition:
   - HH / HV median: range 0.45 / 0.52 dB over the 5 years (at most 0.22 pooled SD).
     2019 HV is the darkest year.
   - alpha: 2019 median 70.5 deg, other years 59.7-63.5 deg (+0.54 SD). The 2019 histogram has no
     45-50 deg peak and has a large mass at 80-90 deg.
   - Surface: 2019 median -16.8 dB, other years -12.9 to -14.3 dB.
   - Dihedral: 2024 has a second mode near -5 dB; 2025 is the lowest year (-0.29 SD).
   - 2019 is the most different year (mean pairwise Wasserstein 0.26-0.38 SD in all pairs with 2019).
     2021 / 2023 / 2025 are the most similar (0.13-0.16 SD).

2. The scene-wide shift does NOT explain the class-share change.
   - Mantel (pairwise PALSAR distance vs pairwise class-share distance): rho is negative,
     k3r -0.49 (p 0.99), k4r -0.41 (p 0.82). Year pairs with more different PALSAR do not have
     more different class shares. 2019 has the most different PALSAR but mid-range class shares.
   - Spearman, PALSAR median shift vs each class share: 1 of 108 tests has p < 0.05
     (k4r C1 vs HH, rho -1.0, p 0.02). At n = 5, about 1.8 such results are expected by chance.
   - Reason: each year's model is trained on the label cells in that year's own images. A shift that
     moves the label cells and the scene together cancels.

3. What can move the class shares is the scene RELATIVE TO the label cells (q_rel: the mainland
   median as a percentile of that year's label cells). Here 2023 stands out:
   - 2023 label cells are brighter (+0.27 to +0.31 SD in HH, HV, Unpolarised; alpha +0.51 SD) and the
     mainland is not. The mainland median sits at the 7th-10th percentile of the label cells
     (other years 11-29).
   - 2023 has the most low classes (k3r no canopy + C1 = 62.6 %) and the least C3 tall open (14.6 %).
     The direction agrees: a scene that is dark relative to the training cells gets lower classes.
   - Over the 5 years, k3r C1 share vs q_rel: rho -0.9 for HH, Dihedral, Unpolarised and alpha
     (p 0.08). Suggestive, not significant.
   - It does not explain 2025: C1 is 44.9 % (nearly as high as 2023) but q_rel is normal (15-20).
     Other sensors are a candidate (S2 Blue is the 1st-2nd gain band in 2024-2025); not tested.

4. At pixel level, class change follows PALSAR change. Between consecutive years 43-54 % (k3r) and
   53-59 % (k4r) of fixed-ground pixels change class. Per pixel, the change in class code (height
   order) correlates with the change in HH, HV, Dihedral, Unpolarised and alpha (Spearman +0.27 to
   +0.54) and with Surface (-0.14 to -0.41). This does not separate real change (fire, growth) from
   acquisition change, and S1 / S2 changes are not controlled for.

Summary: the PALSAR shift is real, but it is mainly a 2019 decomposition anomaly, and it does not
track the class-share change at scene level. The best PALSAR-based lead is the 2023 offset between
the label cells and the rest of the mainland. 2025 is not explained by PALSAR.


METHOD
------

Script     retro_2d\palsar_shift.py (log palsar_shift.log; figure re-run palsar_shift_fig.log)
Bands      feature_stack.load_palsar(): gamma0 HH, HV, Dihedral, Surface, Unpolarised (dB), alpha (deg).
           Same values and grid as the model input.
Area       G:\Australia_shapefile\Dampier_peninsula_mainland_utm51S.gpkg on the 2021 grid
           (44,415,912 px; clip area ratio 1.0000).
Fixed      Every year sampled on the 2021 grid by nearest pixel (2019/2023/2024 offset <= 0.015 px;
ground     2025 is 24.2831 m, offset up to 0.5 px). Kept: px with all 6 bands finite and k3r + k4r
           map valid in all 5 years: 35,097,382 px (79 %).
Shift      median - 5-year mean of medians, in pooled within-year SD.
q_rel      % of the year's k3r label cells below the mainland median.
Tests      Spearman with exact p over all 120 orderings (n = 5; only |rho| = 1 gives p < 0.05).
           Mantel: 10 year pairs, class-share Jensen-Shannon distance vs Wasserstein-1, one-sided.
           Pixel level: 1 M px random sample, consecutive year pairs.

CAVEATS
-------

1. n = 5 years. No scene-level result here is significant; the q_rel lead is a lead only.
2. Only PALSAR is tested. The maps use 20 bands; PALSAR is about half of the gain. S1 and S2 shifts
   are in woody_thickening_splice_1\REPORT_year_signature.md (year signature) but not tested
   against the k3r / k4r shares here.
3. The label cells are small sites (Dampier 2026, Yawuru 2025). Their year-to-year change can be
   real (fire, rain), not only acquisition. The 2023 label-cell offset is not yet explained.
4. 2025 is resampled by nearest to the 2021 grid (up to 0.5 px). This adds co-registration noise
   to the 2024-2025 pixel-level row.
5. Year signature: every cross-year statement is confounded by acquisition differences.
