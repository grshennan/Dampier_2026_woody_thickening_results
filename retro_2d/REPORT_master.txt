REPORT - retro_2d master: Dampier 2019-2025 structure classes
==============================================================
2026-10-30. All retro_2d results in one place.


KEY POINTS
----------
- Own-year models: kappa 0.57-0.62.
- The 2025 model used on earlier years: kappa 0.29-0.43.
- So the 2025 model does NOT transfer to earlier images.
- Cause: the year signature (image shift between years).
- Use the own-year models, not the 2025 model.
- k3r and k4r have about the same kappa.
- k3r has a better weakest class (F1 0.64-0.66 vs 0.53-0.58).
- Map changes between years are NOT a trend.

CAVEATS
-------
1. Year signature: image year is recoverable from the bands.
   Every cross-year comparison is confounded.
2. Labels are 2025/2026 LiDAR. K-fold does not show the
   real accuracy for earlier years.
3. Dampier 2026 clouds are density-filtered; Yawuru not.
   Re-run all when the unfiltered re-export arrives.
4. "none" is 91 % Yawuru cells; tall classes are mostly
   Dampier cells. Part of what the model learns is site.
5. 2025 grid differs (24.28 m vs 24.10 m). Reproject first.
6. YA25_1-5 are single-return LiDAR.
