REPORT: South Dampier, unfiltered vs 5 cm-filtered DJI Terra export
2026-11-03. Script retro_2d\unf_test.py --site "South Dampier". Chain retro_2d\unf_sd_chain.ps1.
Outputs retro_2d\outputs\unf_test_south_dampier\. Same method as REPORT_unf_biota.txt.

RESULT
- Heights do not change: d p98_can median -0.001 m, d p90_can +0.002 m.
- Cover drops: d cover137 median -0.018 (Biota -0.020), in 98.7 % of cells.
- Classes change much more than at Biota:
  k4r 8.1 % (Biota 1.5 %), k3r 5.0 % (Biota 0.9 %), k4h 6.9 % (Biota 1.1 %).
- The Biota per-class estimate for South Dampier (~2 %) was wrong by 4x.
  Cause: at South Dampier 12.6 % of k4r C4 cells go to C2. At Biota that rate was 0.5 %.
- Against the Biota rule ("if a second site changes > 3 %, re-export the others"):
  South Dampier changes 8.1 %, so the filter matters at this site.
  The decision to re-export the remaining sites (Bunda Extra, Country Downs, 9 Mile) is the user's.

GEOREFERENCE
The unfiltered export is the filtered cloud moved by a fixed amount, as at Biota, but by a different amount:
  CHM phase correlation  dx -2.961 m, dy -1.969 m (10 of 10 blocks of 512 m, IQR <= 0.002 m, peak 0.60)
  DEM phase correlation  dx -2.996 m, dy -1.998 m (10 blocks, peak 0.32)
  header min x / y       dx -2.875 m, dy -2.000 m
  (Biota: -1.002 / -1.508, header -1.0 / -1.5)
Header and CHM disagree by 0.086 m in x, so the chain used the measured CHM offset (user: no questions that
night): the height cloud was moved +2.961 / +1.969 m. The DEM points to a round -3.0 / -2.0 m; the 0.04 m
difference is negligible on 20 m cells. User ruling from Biota kept: the old (filtered) position is correct.
=> The DJI Terra "unf" export settings move the cloud by a different amount at each site. Check them before
   any re-export.
