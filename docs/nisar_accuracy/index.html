NISAR accuracy diagnosis — master report

Dampier Peninsula, 2026 four-class (4classTF) classification. Last updated 2026-10-29 (Stages 0, 1, 1.5 and 2 complete). Links open the project files in nisar_2026\. Part of the results site: story.

Executive summary

The 2026 four-class classification (Non-forest, Low forest, Forest, Tall forest) from NISAR L-band and Sentinel-2, trained on the 2026 drone LiDAR at 5 sites on the native 20 m grid, gives a K-fold overall accuracy (OA) of about 0.71. The Yawuru PALSAR-2 work gives 0.77–0.80 (reference only, not directly comparable). This diagnosis tests three hypotheses in order: the NISAR processing (Stage 1), the domain and the diversity of the label sites (Stage 2), and the class thresholds and weights (Stage 3).

Stage 0 measured the noise. Over 6 seeds, OA is 0.715 ± 0.009, so a difference smaller than 0.017 OA (2 SD; kappa 0.019, F1w 0.015) is noise. All earlier 4classTF variants are inside this band. The parameter search optimises a class-balanced score that is almost flat across seeds, while the reported OA moves by 2.5 pp. At the label sites, S2 alone (OA 0.696) is the stronger single source: S2 adds 10.5 pp to NISAR alone (0.610), and NISAR adds 1.9 pp to S2 alone. In the map, the two sources weigh equally: each one-source map is the same distance from the combined map, NISAR sets the Non-forest extent, and the two one-source maps agree on only half of the mainland. The gap to the Yawuru numbers is about 4 noise bands, and NISAR adds much less here than PALSAR-2 added at Yawuru, which supports the Stage 1 hypothesis.

Stage 1 reprocessed NISAR: corrected γ0 / σ0 radiometry, γ0 GSLC backscatter, a GCOV no-phase decomposition, four speckle / decomposition windows, and C-band S1 (CDSE September 2026 mosaic). Coherence was dropped (user: out of scope). None of this raises the NISAR + S2 accuracy beyond the noise (all 0.709–0.726). Only NISAR alone improves: a 9 × 9 Refined Lee filter on GSLC gives +0.038 OA (5 NISAR-only noise bands). S1 adds nothing to S2. The new reference stack is GSLC γ0 + GSLC decomposition, filter 9 × 9, ws 5, + S2 (E:\nisar_2026\feature_stack_2026_gslcg0_rflee9_ws5.tif, tag 4classTF_GSLCg0_f9w5).

Stage 1.5 repeated Stage 0 on that stack. The accuracy did not change (6 seeds, OA 0.717), but the noise band is now 0.006 OA (Stage 0's 0.017 came mostly from one seed). NISAR adds +0.021 OA to S2, the same size as in Stage 0, but now 3.3 bands, so beyond the noise. The two one-source maps still agree on only 51 % of the mainland. The conda environment was recreated during Stage 1 (numpy 2, xgboost 3.4.2); with S2 the effect is 0.0006 OA.

Stage 2 tested the domain and the label sites. The site difference has support; the label quantity does not. Inside Forest only, with 1 km block folds, the features identify the site at OA 0.88 against a prior of 0.50 (S2 alone 0.88). A model trained inside one site is better at the 3 least typical sites (9 Mile and South Dampier, both burnt in 2026; Bunda Extra, white sand): +4.5 to +8.2 noise bands OA. The learning curve is flat at the top (20 k → 34.7 k labels: +0.003 OA). The 5 sites are not spread wider than the 13 Yawuru sites (median 35 against 29 km). Site covariates (rain, soil, elevation, northing, easting) add +0.028 OA in K-fold, but the map shows that they act as site identifiers (straight edges, Non-forest 83 % against 57 %), so they are not for a product. 9 Mile, the weakest site, loses 46 % of its Forest to Low forest: it burnt in July 2026 and many of its labels are near the 2.35 m cut; the LiDAR is not the cause.

Stage 2b tested NAFI fire history (250 m, 2000–2026 from the NAFI WCS, 2026 fires to September) as one extra feature: fire frequency 2017–26 and time since burnt in separate runs (they are correlated, ρ −0.70). Neither raises accuracy beyond the noise (FF +0.006 OA, 1.0 band; TSB −0.002), 9 Mile stays at 0.63, and the maps change little (agreement 0.92–0.93, no artefacts). At 250 m each site has nearly one fire history (9 Mile: one TSB value), so the fire bands are site keys, and a fire effect cannot be separated from the site with 5 sites. Not for the feature set.

Next: Stage 3, class thresholds and weights (PLAN_stage3_thresholds.md (not in this repo)), only when the user says so.

⚑ To come back to (user, 2026-10-28): the S2-only and NISAR-only maps agree on only 51.0 % of the mainland (null 0.332, lift 0.177). Terms

  • 4classTF — Non-forest (< 1.22 m, Low merged in), Low forest (1.22–2.35 m), Forest (2.35–5.381 m), Tall forest (≥ 5.381 m), on the mean of LiDAR p98 per 20 m pixel.
  • K-fold — random stratified 5-fold; optimistic under spatial autocorrelation.
  • Noise band — 2 SD of a metric over the seed runs.
  • Lift — map agreement minus its block-shuffle null (1 km blocks); the informative part of an agreement.
