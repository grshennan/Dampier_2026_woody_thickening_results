Method

  • Rows: 39,023 px; folds folds_cover_height.json: the same as the RF. RF predictions read from disk (no refit).
  • MLP: 49 → h1 → h2 → 1, ReLU, dropout; AdamW, MSE on standardised y; early stop on 15 % of the training fold.
  • Grid: 128-64 / 256-128 / 512-256 × dropout 0.1 / 0.2 / 0.4; best config then seeds 0, 1, 2. Chosen on the test folds (optimistic for the MLP; the RF is not tuned).
  • Maps (made whatever the result): E:\nisar_2026\regression\*_MLP.tif, with RF vs MLP map agreement and an 8 × 8 block-shuffle null.
  • Stratified K-fold only. No leave-site-out.

Context

  • For the classes, MLP = XGB (3 · Classification).
  • For the 2019–2025 canopy-height series, RF vs XGB was a 2–2 split with margins of 2–17 mm: RF stayed primary (chm_regression\ (not in this repo)).
