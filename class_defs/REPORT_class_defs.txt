CAVEATS
-------

- MLP width tested to 1024-512; 256-128 is best. The +0.002 (Dampier) / +0.013 (Yawuru) over 128-64 is above
  2 x seed SD but small.
- Yawuru labels are 12-18 months older than the images; YA25_2/3/5 + govcowwell are single-return clouds.
- XGB search scored blended F1w (both sources); MLP chose its config on blended macro F1.
- No leave-site-out (user rule).
